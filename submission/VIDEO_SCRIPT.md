# Demo video — script (30 seconds or less)

## The rules (from the Space Apps Project Submission Guide)

- **30 seconds or less.** A longer video makes the project ineligible for global judging. Aim for
  28 seconds so an upload never rounds up to 0:31.
- English, or another language with **English subtitles**.
- Hosted outside the Space Apps site (YouTube "Unlisted" or "Public", Google Drive "Anyone with
  the link") and openable **without a login**. Test the link in a private window.
- Nobody under 18 on screen. No music or pictures you do not have the right to use.
- AI-generated pictures or video would need a visible watermark, and AI audio a written note. Use
  your own voice and a real screen recording and none of that applies.
- The judges mostly score the project page; the video counts toward the **Presentation** score.

About 77 spoken words: read it briskly. Record the screen at 1080p with your own voice over it.

## Optional swaps

- **Carbon dioxide instead of WISE 0855** (same eleven seconds): target **A comet's hidden gas**,
  click the doodle, scroll the Object Card to **Spectrum**. “Every object gets a spectrum. This
  comet from another star is sixty-five times brighter exactly where carbon dioxide glows.”
- **A changing star** for the last shot: target **RW Aurigae**, click the doodle, scroll to
  **Brightness at each visit**.
- **A brown dwarf's fingerprint** (three seconds, in place of the Barnard's Star shot): target
  **Epsilon Indi B**, click the doodle, scroll to **Full spectrum**. “One click measures its whole
  spectrum: water and methane, the fingerprint of a brown dwarf.”

## Recording tips

- Rehearse the clicks twice. The saved examples open instantly, so the recording needs no live
  search. Do not type a search during the take: NASA's archive can be slow.
- The landing map needs two seconds to draw. Start recording once the Milky Way is visible.
- Shortcuts: `B` blink, `S` swipe, `D` difference, `T` time-lapse, space to pause.
- Easiest way: record the screen first (`Cmd+Shift+5` → Record Selected Portion), then record the
  voice while watching it, and trim to 28 seconds in iMovie or QuickTime (Edit → Trim).
- Add the two names and “Team KhoFra” as a caption in the first three seconds, and the URL
  `khozhijon.github.io/blinkx` in the last three.
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, title “Blink X — NASA Space Apps 2026 — Team KhoFra”, then
  paste the link into **Link to Project Demo** on the project page.
